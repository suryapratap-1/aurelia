# Aurelia Residences — direction (fictional concept)

Concept: **you read a home before you enter it.** The site moves from the architect's drawing to the lived room: the plan's door swings become the doors you walk through. Could it move to another brand unchanged? No — it only works for one specific plan (Residence 23W) whose walls, doors and areas drive both the drawing and the 3D model.
Family: **world-building** (the development is one live model: city, tower, residence, roof, podium).
Device = **the door-swing arc of an architectural plan** because on every floor plan a door is drawn as a quarter-circle showing how it opens. Appears as: the loader (the entry door arc draws, then the page swings open on its hinge), the cut between outside and inside (a quarter-circle wipe), the front door that opens in 3D as you scroll, the floor plan (every arc is live and focusable), the wordmark (the A's crossbar is a swing arc) and the enquiry button hover.
Medium: **live 3D only** — procedural architecture + CC0 Poly Haven furniture, textures and HDRI; gallery stills are renders of the same model. No AI imagery, no stock photos.
Palette: sampled from the specified materials — travertine ivory #F2EDE4, smoked-oak charcoal #1F1D1A, honed stone #B9AE9C, oak #A47C57, **patinated bronze #8A6A45** (accent: CTA, door arcs, focus ring only).
Type: display **Gloock** (high-contrast serif, the lettering of cut-brass lobby plaques) · text **Schibsted Grotesk** (the plain sans of drawing title blocks). Custom: the wordmark's first A carries a door-swing arc instead of a crossbar.
Voice: like **the project architect walking a buyer through on a site visit** — names the stone, the hinge, the hour the sun reaches the table. Says: "The front door is 2.7 m of solid walnut on three bronze hinges." "At 16:50 in late October the sun clears the roofline across the river and lands on the dining table." "Every bedroom has a window; two have two." Never: "Elevate your lifestyle." "Luxury redefined." "Your dream home awaits."
Motion personality: **weighted, hinged, unhurried** (doors, not springs). Transition grammar: **the swing** — a quarter-circle reveal about a hinge corner, used by the loader, the outside→inside cut and section reveals.
AI-tell exception (max 1): **level numbers as chapter markers** (L23, Roof, L4) because a tower numbers itself.

## Brand bible (all facts fictional placeholders for a concept)

Aurelia Residences is a fictional 42-level tower on Ostra Quay, a fictional riverside district. It stands on a three-level podium; the podium roof (Level 4) holds a garden and a glazed gym. The roof holds a 20 m pool along the west parapet, a glazed lounge and a terrace under a bronze pergola. Floor-to-floor height is 3.4 m; apartments have 3.0 m clear ceilings. The tower faces west across a 400 m bend of the Ostra river, so nothing is built between the living rooms and the sunset. Residence 23W occupies the west half of Level 23: 288 m² internal across three bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms and an open living, dining and kitchen floor 20 m long, plus a 22 m² balcony. Glass runs floor to ceiling on three sides. The front door is solid walnut, 2.7 m tall, hung on three bronze hinges. Floors are oak herringbone, 70 × 420 mm blocks; the wet rooms are honed grey limestone; the kitchen island is a 2.6 m slab of white marble with waterfall ends; joinery is smoked walnut. The primary suite runs through a dressing room into a bath with a freestanding tub under the north window. The second bedroom is the corner room, with windows west and south. Late in October the sun clears the roofline across the river at about 16:50 and reaches the dining table. Every number on the site comes from one plan file, so the drawing, the model and the schedule agree.
